## Orientation

Intervals describe ranges, but their endpoints have no useful meaning until the contract says whether those endpoints are included. This chapter begins with safe endpoint ordering and then separates closed, open, and half-open boundary rules. Later lessons use those rules for merging, insertion, two-list intersection, compatibility decisions, and event sweeps.

You should already know arrays, object comparators, sorting, and two-pointer scans. A heap-based room allocator is deliberately deferred to Chapter 17; intervals supply the release times, while the heap supplies the reusable-resource invariant.
